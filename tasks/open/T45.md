# T45: Categorize real yt-dlp errors correctly

**Priority:** Medium. Validated defect from the 2026-11-14 review.

Evidence: reproduced at `4303fe5` by calling `categories.categorize` on `yt_dlp.utils.DownloadError` instances. A private video's real message is categorized `blocked` instead of `unavailable`: the locked yt-dlp appends its login hint, "Use --cookies-from-browser or --cookies for the authentication…", to every reason containing "sign in" (`extractor/youtube/_video.py`, the `'sign in' in reason.lower()` branch), and the `cookies` pattern is tried first. The existing test at `tests/test_categories.py` passes only because its message is truncated before that hint. A raw `ssl.SSLError` or `urllib.error.URLError` in the cause chain is categorized `filesystem`, because both are `OSError` subclasses and reach the catch-all. Premieres and upcoming live events ("Premieres in 3 hours", "This live event will begin in 2 days.") fall to `internal`. Provenance of those inputs: the login-hint branch and the hint's text were read in the locked source, but the reason texts themselves ("Private video. Sign in if you've been granted access to this video", "Premieres in 3 hours", "This live event will begin in 2 days.") come from YouTube's responses and were written from memory, not captured from a live run. Not reproduced, hypothesis only: an unrelated `OSError` reached through `__context__` could mislabel a failure as `filesystem`.

Outcome: `category` is a trustworthy hint again, which matters more once stale cookies are surfaced through `blocked` failures (C56): private, removed and members-only videos are `unavailable` even with the login hint attached; genuine sign-in, bot-check and age-confirmation errors stay `blocked`; TLS and URL errors are `network`; scheduled premieres and live events get a deliberate category, proposed `unavailable`, stated as an assumption. The raw message stays authoritative and unchanged (DESIGN 5.9).

Direction: build the test corpus from complete messages as the locked yt-dlp composes them, citing the source location of each, not from hand-shortened strings; later live runs under [T25](T25.md) and [T49](T49.md) add verbatim captures. Decide explicitly whether the generic `cookies` pattern should exist at all, given that the hint mentions cookies on errors that cookies cannot fix.

Paths: `youtube_collector/categories.py`, `tests/test_categories.py`, DESIGN 5.9 if the category descriptions change. Compatibility surface: the `category` value of some failures changes; the set of categories does not, unless the executor proposes one and the user accepts it.

Validation: the corpus above, including the three reproduced cases, failing before the change and passing after; the existing typed-error cases still pass. Dependencies: none; independent of every other open task.
