## Implementation

- **`youtube_collector/responses.py`:** pydantic models for jobs, items, failures, the two lists and health. Extra fields are forbidden, and the store's enums are reused.
- **`youtube_collector/api.py`:** each route names its model; routes returning items use `response_model_exclude_unset`, so an item keeps omitting artifacts its job does not request while unknown outcomes stay null. `/health` declares its model for both 200 and 503 and still returns its body directly.
- **Documentation:** DESIGN sections 6 and 14.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_api.py` checks the published fields and each endpoint's schema; `tests/test_responses.py` checks that undeclared fields and values outside the enums are refused.
