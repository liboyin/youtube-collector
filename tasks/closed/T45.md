A private video's real message is categorized `blocked` instead of `unavailable`: the locked yt-dlp appends its login hint, "Use --cookies-from-browser or --cookies for the authentication…", to every reason containing "sign in" (`extractor/youtube/_video.py`, the `'sign in' in reason.lower()` branch), and the `cookies` pattern is tried first. The existing test at `tests/test_categories.py` passes only because its message is truncated before that hint. A raw `ssl.SSLError` or `urllib.error.URLError` in the cause chain is categorized `filesystem`, because both are `OSError` subclasses and reach the catch-all. Premieres and upcoming live events ("Premieres in 3 hours", "This live event will begin in 2 days.") fall to `internal`. Provenance of those inputs: the login-hint branch and the hint's text were read in the locked source, but the reason texts themselves ("Private video. Outcome: `category` is a trustworthy hint again, which matters more once stale cookies are surfaced through `blocked` failures (C56): private, removed and members-only videos are `unavailable` even with the login hint attached; genuine sign-in, bot-check and age-confirmation errors stay `blocked`; TLS and URL errors are `network`; scheduled premieres and live events get a deliberate category, proposed `unavailable`, stated as an assumption. ## Implementation

`youtube_collector/categories.py`: typed rules for yt-dlp's `CookieLoadError` (`blocked`), `GeoRestrictedError` (`unavailable`) and `networking.exceptions.TransportError` (`network`), and for `ssl.SSLError` and `urllib.error.URLError` (`network`) ahead of the `OSError` catch-all. The walk along the error's chain stops at an HTTP error that carries a status (yt-dlp's `HTTPError`, `urllib.error.HTTPError`), so the message, which names the status, decides. The generic `cookies` message pattern is gone; `captcha` and "ip is likely being blocked" are `blocked`, and "premieres in" and "live event will begin" are `unavailable`. The `Category` docstring and DESIGN 5.9 describe `unavailable` and `blocked` accordingly. `tests/test_categories.py` gains the corpus described in the boundary.
